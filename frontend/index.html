Project Review & Defense Summary (How to present this to Examiners):

“We evaluated three distinct retrieval strategies: Exhaustive Flat Scan establishes exact ground-truth retrieval, Static IVF represents conventional cluster-partitioned indexing (77.4% reduction at 6.13/10 accuracy), and our proposed Adaptive AI Multi-Tier introduces dynamic search-space reduction and confidence-aware gating. In aggressive mode, Adaptive achieves 88.0% pruning examining only 180 images. When configured with recall-aware confidence gating (τ = 0.82–0.85), it dynamically escalates from HOT into WARM tier whenever candidate support is sparse, achieving 5.80/10 Top-10 accuracy with 60–64% pruning and sub-0.06 ms latency. As repository size scales from 1,500 to 100,000 images, Adaptive yields up to 28.3× speedup over linear scan.”
